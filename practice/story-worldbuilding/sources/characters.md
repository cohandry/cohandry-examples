# Characters
## Mira
Apprentice tidekeeper, age 24. Observant and impatient with ceremony. Wants to warn the ferry captain. Knows the basin rule but does not know who loosened the drain valve.
## Oren
Retired keeper, age 61. Knows the valve was deliberately loosened, but has not told Mira. Remains in the tower during this scene.
## Captain Vale
Waits at the ferry until 18:00. Has never seen the basin. Do not give Mira access to Oren’s private knowledge without a scene event.
