# World bible
## Setting
Tern Quay is a fictional tidal harbor. The footpath to the lighthouse floods at 18:10.
## Lantern rule
The blue lantern is bolted inside the tower and cannot be carried away. It shines only while seawater touches its copper basin. No portable duplicate exists in established canon.
## Timeline
17:40 Mira reaches the harbor steps. 18:00 the last ferry departs. 18:10 the footpath floods.
## Tone
Quiet wonder, concrete coastal details, no graphic violence. Technology is hand-built and mechanical; no phones or electricity.
