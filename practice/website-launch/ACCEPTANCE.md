# Acceptance checks

- Title, date, time, location and all three sessions match the sources.
- Clear RSVP email link; no claim that a form stores registrations.
- Layout works at 360px and 1280px without horizontal scrolling.
- Logical headings, visible focus, usable keyboard navigation and readable contrast.
- Owner or configured browser verification is recorded; missing checks are disclosed.

Record commands actually run, their outcomes and manual checks. Do not substitute an agent assertion for observed evidence.
